Face’s Music Party is a Nickelodeon children’s music variety series produced by Jonas & Co., featuring the iconic Nick Jr. character Face alongside animated and live-action segments.

For this project, I worked as a 2D character rigger, creating rigs for dozens of characters in After Effects. Each character came with its own challenges, requiring different approaches to build flexible and animation-ready rigs while preserving the original artwork and style.

Working across such a wide variety of characters was both challenging and enjoyable, giving me extensive experience in 2D character rigging, deformation, and animation-ready character setups in After Effects.
