A technical tutorial I created to show how to rig a character’s jaw for lip sync in After Effects using Joysticks 'n Sliders, including a setup for multiple facial controls.

The tutorial came from a problem I had encountered myself while working with character rigs. After finding a solution, I decided to share it with other animators who might be facing the same challenge.

The tutorial was featured by aescripts and shared by several animation and After Effects communities on Instagram, helping it reach a wider audience of artists and animators.
