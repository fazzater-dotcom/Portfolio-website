A personal 2D animation inspired by one of Morocco’s most iconic dishes: the tagine. The idea was to turn the familiar anticipation of a delicious meal into a playful horror-comedy twist.

The title “Tajinn” is a play on the words tagine and jinn, referencing the supernatural creatures found in Islamic tradition.

I created the initial rough animation in Toon Boom, working through the movement and action frame by frame, then brought it into After Effects for the final look, compositing, effects, and polish.
