Lay Chafi is a surreal and humorous music video by Moroccan punk-rock band Betweenatna, blending music, comedy, and exaggerated visual situations.

I worked as VFX Supervisor, overseeing the visual effects and also contributing directly to the VFX work. My responsibilities included compositing, visual effects, and animation, helping integrate and enhance the visual elements throughout the video.

The project gave me the opportunity to work across different VFX techniques while contributing to the distinctive and playful visual style of the music video.
