A music video project for Moroccan DJ and producer Mr ID, known for blending contemporary electronic sounds with Moroccan and African musical influences.

I worked primarily on compositing, VFX, and animation, integrating visual effects and animated elements into the footage to enhance the atmosphere and bring the music's visual identity to life.

The project allowed me to combine VFX, compositing, and 2D animation to create a cohesive and dynamic visual experience.
