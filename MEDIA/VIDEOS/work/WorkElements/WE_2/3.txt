NoVir is a scientific project focused on developing broad-spectrum antiviral solutions designed to provide protection against a range of viral threats and contribute to better pandemic preparedness.

For this project, I was responsible for developing the storyboard from the provided script and bringing the entire visual concept to life through animation and motion design.

I translated the scientific information into a clear and engaging visual narrative, designing the composition, transitions, movement, and pacing to make complex concepts easier to understand. From the initial storyboard through to the final animation, I worked on shaping the visual storytelling and creating a cohesive motion language for the project.

The final piece combines 2D animation, motion design, and visual storytelling to communicate a complex scientific subject in an accessible and engaging way.
