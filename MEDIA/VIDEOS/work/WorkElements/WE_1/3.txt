PartnerGuard™ — AI-Powered Relationship Damage Control

What if your phone could recognize your partner and automatically protect the things you probably don't want them to see?

Meet PartnerGuard™ — a fictional SaaS product designed for one very specific problem: relationship damage control.

This personal motion design project explores how a SaaS product can communicate its idea through UI animation, storytelling, visual effects, and a little bit of absurd humor.

Features include:
• Partner face recognition
• Personalized privacy profiles
• Selective protection for messages, photos and search history
• Automatic privacy activation
• Dual-PIN access
• Discreet background operation

Created as a personal project to explore SaaS / product animation, UI motion design, 2D animation and VFX.
