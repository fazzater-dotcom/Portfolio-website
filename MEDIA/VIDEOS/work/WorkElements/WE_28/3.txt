This animation started as a simple practice project.

I had been seeing a few animation styles using grids, and I really liked the visual possibilities they offered. So I decided to experiment and create something of my own.

Then I ran into a problem: creating and animating those grids manually in After Effects was a real pain.

I looked for a solution and found a paid plugin that could help. But instead of buying it, I thought: why not try making my own?

So I spent two days building a custom plugin, then used it to create this animation.

What started as a practice piece ended up becoming two projects in one: building a tool and then using it to bring the animation to life.

Definitely a fun little experiment.
