LIFE Giga Regio Factory is a European project focused on accelerating the transition toward large-scale, high-performance and Net Zero-energy building renovations through industrialised renovation solutions.

For this project, I was responsible for developing the storyboard from the provided script and producing the final animation and motion design.

The challenge was to translate a complex subject involving energy-efficient renovations, prefabricated solutions, industrialisation, and large-scale building transformation into a clear and engaging visual narrative. I developed the visual flow and storytelling through the storyboard, then brought the concepts to life through 2D animation, motion design, transitions, and visual explanations.

The final animation combines informative storytelling with a clean visual language, making a complex sustainability and construction-related topic easier to understand and more engaging for the audience.
