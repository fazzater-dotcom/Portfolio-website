HYNNOVA is a renewable energy project focused on developing advanced solutions for the modular, efficient, and safe production, compression, and storage of renewable hydrogen.

For this project, I was responsible for developing the storyboard from the provided script and creating the final animation and motion design.

The challenge was to translate a highly technical subject involving hydrogen production, energy systems, storage, and industrial infrastructure into a clear and engaging visual narrative. I developed the storyboard to establish the visual flow and storytelling, then brought the concepts to life through 2D animation, motion design, transitions, and visual explanations.

The final piece uses animation and motion design to make complex renewable-energy technologies more accessible, while maintaining a clean and cohesive visual language throughout the project.
