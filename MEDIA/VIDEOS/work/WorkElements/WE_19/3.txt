Lotus Evija — VFX

The Lotus Evija is an all-electric hypercar designed to push the boundaries of performance and technology. For its launch film, The Creator, I contributed to the VFX work, helping bring the car’s powerful and futuristic identity to life.

My work focused on creating and integrating dynamic lighting effects, energy-driven visuals, and atmospheric particle effects throughout the film. The goal was to enhance the feeling of electricity and power surrounding the Evija while keeping the effects seamlessly integrated with the live-action footage.

The result is a visual language that reinforces the idea of the Evija being brought to life through energy — combining precision, technology, and cinematic VFX to elevate the final film.
